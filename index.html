📖 Abstract & Core Thesis

Generative policies (such as Diffusion Policy and Flow Matching) have excelled at robot imitation learning. However, both paradigms fundamentally treat control as statistical trajectory regression: their velocity and score fields learn empirical averages over offline demonstrations. When applied to dynamically constrained and underactuated systems, statistical interpolation violates dynamic constraints, leading to catastrophic failure under out-of-distribution disturbances.

Crucially, stability is not a 1-dimensional trajectory; it is an open topological neighborhood (invariant set / basin of attraction).

We propose Model-Driven Diffusion, decoupling generative control into a Topological-Metric Duality:

  • Topology via Controller: A physics-grounded controller defines the contractive vector field over the phase-space neighborhood, ensuring structural Lyapunov stability ($\dot{V} \le 0$). When noise occurs, the response may be dynamically sub-optimal, but stability is preserved as a hard invariant.
  • Metric via Demonstrations: Within this topologically stable funnel, diffusion/flow matching learns the Riemannian metric—optimizing multi-modal paths, velocities, timings, and task-specific styles demonstrated by humans.

Scientific Finding — The Physical Bound vs. The Data Bound:

Expanding data from 1,000 to 3,000 trajectories enables Flow Matching to reach 100% recovery at low noise, perfectly replicating the optimal controller. However, under extreme disturbances ($\sigma \ge 0.25\text{ rad}$), Flow Matching plateaus at exactly ~59% and ~38%, matching the physical ceiling of the model-driven controller.

This proves that the bottleneck at high noise is the physical basin of attraction of underactuated dynamics under torque saturation ($|u| \le 15\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$), rather than empirical dataset scarcity.

  • Model-Driven Topology Flow (Ours - 48.0% success): Even though no human ever demonstrated how to recover from this extreme state, the controller's continuous contractive vector field ($\dot{V} \le 0$) activates automatically, arresting the fall and curving the trajectory back into the stable upright equilibrium!

⚙️ Mathematical Formulation

The planar underactuated double pendulum (Pendubot) dynamics satisfy Euler-Lagrange equations:

$$M(q)\ddot{q} + C(q, \dot{q})\dot{q} + G(q) = B u, \quad B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

where $q = [\theta_1, \theta_2]^T$ and single motor input is bounded by $|u| \le u_{max}$.

We cast the three paradigms into a unified continuous vector-field restoration process $\dot{x} = \mathbf{v}(x)$:

  • Method 1 (Score Diffusion): $\mathbf{v}_{Score}(x) = -\frac{1}{2}\beta x - \frac{1}{2}g^2 \nabla_x \log p(x)$ (Empirical statistical density score)
  • Method 2 (Flow Matching): $\mathbf{v}_{CFM}(x) = \mathbb{E}[x^* - x_{pert} \mid x]$ (Empirical transport average)
  • Method 3 (Model-Driven Diffusion): $\mathbf{v}_{MPC}(x) = f(x, u^*_{MPC}(x))$ (Physical optimal control dynamics)
