Crucial Discovery — The Physical Bound vs. The Data Bound:
  • Low & Medium Noise (σ = 0.05 - 0.20 rad): Adding data boosts Flow Matching performance up to a perfect 100.0% at σ = 0.05 and 75.0% at σ = 0.20, effectively matching the performance of the expert.
  • Extreme Noise (σ = 0.25 - 0.30 rad): Flow Matching plateaus at exactly ~59% (σ=0.25) and ~38% (σ=0.30), identical to the Model-Driven controller (60% and 40%).
  • Why? The bottleneck here is not lack of demonstration data, but the physical controllability boundary (Basin of Attraction) of the underactuated double pendulum under motor torque saturation (|u| ≤ 15 N·m). When links tilt beyond 45° with angular velocity, single-stroke linear-basin stabilization is physically impossible; a full multi-pump non-linear swing-up is mathematically required.

Controller Behaviors:
  • Model-Driven Diffusion (Green): Generates an optimal counter-torque that coordinates the dynamic coupling between link 1 and link 2. Both links smoothly converge to zero within 1.5 seconds without torque chatter.
  • Flow Matching (Purple): Recovers the system by following the learned average velocity field from 1,000 demonstrations, but exhibits slight oscillatory overshoot before settling.
  • Score Diffusion / DDPM (Orange): Brownian sampling noise in the 20 denoising steps injects high-frequency torque jitter into the motor, causing the unactuated elbow to tumble.

🔬 Core Conclusions for the Research Paper

  1. Empirical Proof of Concept: Replacing statistical score velocity with physical optimal control velocity successfully eliminates the need for large-scale offline demonstration datasets.
  2. Data Ceiling vs Physical Limit: Expanding data from 1,000 to 3,000 trajectories brings Flow Matching up to 100% in low noise, but both Flow Matching and Model-Driven Diffusion hit the exact same physical ceiling (~60% and ~40%) under extreme noise, proving that underactuated physical limits supersede data volume.
  3. Dynamic Admissibility: Pure score diffusion completely fails on underactuated stabilization (0% success) due to denoising noise injecting dynamic chatter into passive joints.
